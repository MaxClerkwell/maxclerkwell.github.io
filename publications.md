# Publications, Patents & Software

Canonical list of scholarly output by **Stephan Bökelmann**
([ORCID 0000-0002-2119-0064](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-2119-0064) ·
[INSPIRE-HEP](https://inspirehep.net/authors/2177110) ·
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/a/0000-0002-2119-0064.html)).

## Peer-reviewed publications

The PANDA papers below were written during my time in the
[PANDA Collaboration](https://panda.gsi.de/) at FAIR, where I work on the
luminosity detector and HV-MAPS sensor characterisation.

- **PANDA Phase One** — *The European Physical Journal A* (2021).
  DOI: [10.1140/epja/s10050-021-00475-y](https://doi.org/10.1140/epja/s10050-021-00475-y)
- **Study of excited Ξ baryons with the PANDA detector** — *EPJ A* (2021).
  DOI: [10.1140/epja/s10050-021-00444-5](https://doi.org/10.1140/epja/s10050-021-00444-5)
- **The potential of Λ and Ξ⁻ studies with PANDA at FAIR** — *EPJ A* (2021).
  DOI: [10.1140/epja/s10050-021-00386-y](https://doi.org/10.1140/epja/s10050-021-00386-y)
- **Feasibility studies for the measurement of time-like proton electromagnetic form factors** — *EPJ A* (2021).
  DOI: [10.1140/epja/s10050-020-00333-3](https://doi.org/10.1140/epja/s10050-020-00333-3)
- **Precision resonance energy scans with the PANDA experiment at FAIR** — *EPJ A* (2019).
  DOI: [10.1140/epja/i2019-12718-2](https://doi.org/10.1140/epja/i2019-12718-2)
- **Microfabricated Electrochemical Sensors as a Sentinel System to Detect Biofilms in River Systems** — *ECS Meeting Abstracts* (2025).
  DOI: [10.1149/ma2025-02632961mtgabs](https://doi.org/10.1149/ma2025-02632961mtgabs)

## Patents

- **Leiterplattengehäusevorrichtung** (printed-circuit-board housing device) —
  German patent **DE 10 2020 128 143**, granted by the DPMA in January 2026,
  patent specification published 2026. Filed 2020. Related German utility model
  (Gebrauchsmuster) *Leiterplatten-Gehäuse mit integrierter Sensorik*.
  The enclosureless-PCB concept behind it is described in my writing on
  enclosureless PCB design.
- **OmnAIScope** — German national phase of a PCT application (filed 2019,
  examination ongoing at the DPMA) covering the OmnAI automotive measurement
  scope developed with Auto-Intern GmbH.

## Open-source software

Selected projects, with my role:

- [skunkforce/edgy_boards](https://github.com/skunkforce/edgy_boards) — open
  hardware: single-function PCB modules on a standard edge connector. Author
  and maintainer.
- [omnai-project/OmnAIView](https://github.com/omnai-project/OmnAIView) —
  time-series visualisation front end for OmnAI DAQ devices. Project lead.
- [STEMgraph](https://github.com/STEMgraph) — graph-structured, self-contained
  engineering exercises used in my teaching. Founder and maintainer.
- [MaxClerkwell/ax7020-bringup](https://github.com/MaxClerkwell/ax7020-bringup)
  — mainline U-Boot bring-up of the ALINX AX7020 over JTAG and Ethernet.
  Author.
- [kvasir-io/Kvasir](https://github.com/kvasir-io/Kvasir) — C++
  metaprogramming library for zero-cost register abstraction in embedded
  software. Contributor (project by Odin Holmes).
