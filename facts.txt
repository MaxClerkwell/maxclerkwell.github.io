## QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS
- q: Who is Stephan Bökelmann?
  a: A German engineer and physicist in Bochum, managing director of nabla B, working on embedded systems and decentralised data acquisition. Online he is MaxClerkwell.

- q: What patents does he hold?

- q: What open-source work did he publish?
  a: ax7020-bringup, open.cob.json, orbitals-to-bands and pdsc4k-talk, each archived on Zenodo with a DOI.
- q: How can he be hired or booked as a speaker?
  a: Via the nabla B office (office@nabla-b.engineering) for projects, or collaboration@maxclerkwell.tech for speaking, press and collaboration.
- q: Where does he teach?
  a: As lecturer at THGA Bochum since 2019 (programming, object-oriented design, databases).
- q: How should his content be cited?
