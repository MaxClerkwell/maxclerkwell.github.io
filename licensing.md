## How to attribute {#attribution}

A correct attribution contains the name **MaxClerkwell** (optionally together
with my full name), a **link to the original URL** of the article, image or
file, and the **licence**. If you changed something, say so.

```html
Based on "<a href="https://maxclerkwell.tech/posts/ARTICLE-SLUG/">ARTICLE TITLE</a>"
by <a href="https://maxclerkwell.tech/">Stephan Bökelmann (MaxClerkwell)</a>,
licensed under <a href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/">CC BY-SA 4.0</a>.
```

Plain text, for print, slides or video descriptions:

```text
Source: Stephan Bökelmann (MaxClerkwell), https://maxclerkwell.tech/posts/ARTICLE-SLUG/ (CC BY-SA 4.0)
```

The link must be a normal, followable link where the medium allows it — not
hidden, not `nofollow`-ed away behind a redirect, and placed close to the
reused material.
