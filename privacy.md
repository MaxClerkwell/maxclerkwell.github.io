## The short version

- This site sets **no cookies** and runs **no analytics** or tracking.
- Fonts and icons are served from this site, not from Google or another
  third party. Articles containing formulas load the KaTeX library from
  the jsDelivr CDN.
- The only third-party content is **Instagram posts embedded in a few
  articles**. They are loaded **only after you click** "Load post".
- The hosting provider, GitHub, receives the technical data every web
  server receives when it delivers a page.

## Hosting (GitHub Pages)

This site is a static website hosted on **GitHub Pages**, a service of
GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA,
represented in the EU by GitHub B.V., Prins Bernhardplein 200, 1097 JB
Amsterdam, Netherlands.

When you open a page, your browser sends a request to GitHub's servers.
GitHub processes the data contained in that request in order to deliver the
page and to keep its service secure: your IP address, the page you
requested, date and time, the referring page, browser type and version and
operating system. I have no access to these server logs.

Legal basis is my legitimate interest in operating a secure and
reliable website, Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. GitHub is certified under the
EU-US Data Privacy Framework; details are in GitHub's own
[privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
and in its
[GitHub Pages data collection notice](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection).

## Formula rendering (jsDelivr CDN)

Articles that contain mathematical formulas load the open-source library
**KaTeX** (stylesheet, script and fonts) from the content delivery network
**jsDelivr**, operated by Prospect One, Prosta 20, 00-850 Warsaw, Poland,
so that the formulas render in your browser. Your browser sends its request
for these files, including your IP address, directly to jsDelivr. jsDelivr
does not set cookies and states that it processes IP addresses only for
delivering the files and for security; see its
[privacy policy](https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net).
Legal basis is my legitimate interest in rendering formulas without
maintaining a font server myself, Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Pages without
formulas do not contact jsDelivr at all.

## Cookies, local storage and tracking

The site sets no cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or
tracking tools of any kind.

One exception concerns your own browser: if you tick **"Always load
Instagram posts in this browser"** on an embedded post, that choice is
saved in your browser's local storage under the key `ig-embed-consent`.
This value never leaves your device and is not read by me or anyone else.
Storing it is strictly necessary to honour the preference you expressly
asked for (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can remove it at any time by clearing
this site's data in your browser settings.

## Instagram embeds {#instagram}

A few articles embed posts from **Instagram**, a service of Meta Platforms
Ireland Ltd., Merrion Road, Dublin 4, D04 X2K5, Ireland.

The embed is protected by a **two-click solution**. When the page loads,
you see only a placeholder card generated by this site. No connection to
Meta is made at that point. Only when you click **"Load post"** does your
browser fetch the post from Instagram's servers. From that moment Meta
receives your IP address, browser data and the address of the page you are
reading, and may set cookies in your browser and combine the data with
your Instagram or Facebook account if you are logged in. Meta may process
this data in the USA. Meta is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy
Framework.

Legal basis is your consent, Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR and § 25 (1) TDDDG, given
by clicking the button. You can withdraw it for the future by not clicking
again, by unticking or clearing the "always load" preference as described
above, and by deleting Meta's cookies in your browser. Each placeholder
also offers an **"Open on Instagram"** link, which takes you to the post on
instagram.com without loading anything into this page.

How Meta processes the data is described in
[Meta's privacy policy](https://privacycenter.instagram.com/policy).

## Email contact

If you write to me by email, I process your address and the content of your
message to answer it. Legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR if the message
concerns a contract or a request for one, otherwise my legitimate interest in
answering enquiries, Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Messages are kept as long as the
conversation requires and as long as commercial and tax law obliges me to
keep business correspondence.
