# Talks & Conference Appearances

Talks and conference appearances by **Stephan Bökelmann** (MaxClerkwell).
Recordings, where they exist, live on the organisers' channels.

## Conference talks

- **"Poor Man's Introduction to Wire-Bonding"** — given twice in 2025: at
  [KiCon Europe 2025](https://kicon.kicad.org/europe2025/), UFO,
  Ruhr-Universität Bochum, 12 September 2025
  ([programme entry](https://pretalx.kicad.org/kicon-europe-2025/talk/DKJB9Q/)),
  and at
  [KiCon Asia 2025](https://kicon.kicad.org/asia2025/),
  Shenzhen, 15 November 2025
  ([programme](https://kicon.kicad.org/asia2025/en/schedule/talks/)). Two years of hands-on wire bonding of CLICpix
  and MuPix detector chips on a Delvotec bonder, in 25 minutes.
  [Recording on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dNBwY7L6niI).
- **"Everything, Everywhere, All at Once"** — keynote at
  [PDSC4K, Practical Data Science Congress 4000](https://skunkforce.org/pdsc4k/),
  ZESS, Ruhr-Universität Bochum, 22 May 2026. The four zones of a real data
  pipeline, from analog front-end to Provider API;
  [write-up on this blog](/posts/pdsc4k-may-2026/).
- **"RTCS"** — [emBO++ 2024](https://embo.io/programm-2024/), Bochum,
  March 2024. Plus the conference opening on both days and
  ["Roast for Clarify"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wCL-0NgVf3I)
  on the *Introduction to Programming* course at THGA Bochum (recording on
  YouTube).
- **"Wer viel misst, misst Mist? Ein Plädoyer für modernere Datenanbindung
  von Messgeräten im Namen der Industrie 4.0"** —
  [Data Science Ruhrgebiet Kongress 2019](https://data-science.ruhr/speaker/stephan-boekelmann/),
  Bochum.

## PANDA collaboration meetings (2019–2025)

Progress talks on parts of the PANDA luminosity detector at the
[PANDA Collaboration](https://panda.gsi.de/) meetings at GSI Darmstadt,
held roughly three times a year (talks are collaboration-internal, not
public): CM 19/1 (Mar 2019), 19/3 (Nov 2019), 2020-III (online),
21/1–21/3 (2021, online), 22/1 (May/Jun 2022), 22/3 (Oct 2022),
23/1–23/3 (2023), 24/2 (Jun 2024), and subsequent meetings through 2025.

## Workshops & teaching appearances

- **"Plötzlich Chef*in. Vom Gründen zur Führung"** — leadership workshop for
  founders, [Hochschule Bochum / TZR](https://www.hochschule-bochum.de/en/news/n/workshop-ploetzlich-chefin/),
  27 October 2025 (instructor).

## Organiser roles

- [emBO++](https://embo.io/) — International Technical Symposium for Embedded
  Systems Development, Bochum. Principal organiser **since 2015**.
- [KiCon Europe](https://kicon.kicad.org/) — KiCad conference. Organiser
  **since 2020**, host of KiCon Europe 2024 in Bochum.
- [PDSC4K](https://skunkforce.org/pdsc4k/) — Practical Data Science Congress,
  Bochum. Co-organiser **since 2024** (via open Skunkforce e.V.).
- [pottCPP](https://www.meetup.com/pottcpp/) — Ruhr C++ User Group. Organiser
  **since 2017**.
- IHK Mittleres Ruhrgebiet — examination board for IT specialists
  (Fachinformatiker), board member **since 2020**.
